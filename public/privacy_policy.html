Privacy Policy

Tipitakapali.org is committed to protecting your privacy across our mobile apps (Tipitaka CST), computer apps (Tipitakapali.org) and website — collectively, the “Service”.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, or location.
  • Our focus is on ensuring that your personal privacy is maintained while still delivering a high-quality service.

2. Information We Do Collect

To keep the online dictionary API server running smoothly and to improve performance, our dictionary API server may log anonymized text queries sent to it. This includes:

  • Pāḷi Statistics: Information such as the statistics of Pāḷi words, including which Pāḷi text files are opened and which words are looked up on our dictionary server.
  • Aggregated Data: Data is collected in a manner that does not identify you personally.

3. How We Use the Information

  • Performance Improvement: To monitor and analyze the performance and usage of the app.
  • Service Enhancement: To improve functionality and user experience.
  • Security & Maintenance: To help ensure the ongoing security and stability of our Service.

4. Data Security

  • We take reasonable measures to protect the data we collect against unauthorized access or disclosure.
  • However, please note that no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Our Service may include links or integrations with third-party services. We do not control these services, and their privacy practices may differ from ours.
  • We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party providers.

6. Children’s Privacy

We do not collect any personal data from users; therefore, provisions regarding children’s privacy do not apply.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time.
  • Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated “Effective Date.” We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
