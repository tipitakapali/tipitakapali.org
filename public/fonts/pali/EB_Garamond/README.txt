EB Garamond Font Family
========================

EB Garamond is a classical Claude Garamont serif revival designed by Georg Duffner
and expanded by Octavio Pardo. It faithfully reflects the master typeface cut
by Claude Garamont in the 16th century, providing warm, humanistic proportions
and exceptional reading legibility.

Key Features:
- Classical renaissance serif aesthetics.
- Extensive coverage of Latin Extended Additional glyphs, ensuring support
  for Pāḷi Roman transliteration letters (ṃ, ṁ, ā, ī, ū, ṭ, ḍ, ṇ, ṅ, ñ, ḷ, etc.).

Styles Included:
- EBGaramond-Regular.woff2 / .woff
- EBGaramond-Bold.woff2 / .woff
- EBGaramond-Italic.woff2 / .woff
- EBGaramond-BoldItalic.woff2 / .woff
