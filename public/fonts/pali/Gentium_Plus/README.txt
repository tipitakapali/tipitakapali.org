Gentium Plus Font Family
=========================

Gentium Plus is a typeface family designed to enable the diverse typographic
traditions of Latin-, Cyrillic-, and Greek-based scripts to be represented
with clarity and elegance. It is particularly renowned for its extensive
support of complex linguistic diacritics, scholarly publishing, and
Pāḷi / Sanskrit Roman transliteration (IAST).

Key Features:
- Complete Unicode Latin Extended Additional support, ensuring full coverage
  of Pāḷi Roman transliteration letters (ṃ, ṁ, ā, ī, ū, ṭ, ḍ, ṇ, ṅ, ñ, ḷ, etc.).
- Compact line metrics and graceful serifs optimized for long-form canonical
  reading.
